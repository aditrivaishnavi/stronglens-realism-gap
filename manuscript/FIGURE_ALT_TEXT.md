# Figure alt text for submission

## Data preparation, training, and evaluation roles

Two linked flow diagrams. The first tracks catalogue positives to 63
confirmed systems and tracks 453,100 sampled negatives to 409,551 retained
rows and grouped data partitions. The second shows noisy-label mixtures
feeding the primary epoch-160 detector and a 12-run sensitivity panel,
followed by separate calibration thresholds and common frozen evaluation
populations.

## Final paired source construction

Two rows compare the structured source and its smooth single-Sérsic twin. The
first column shows similar but visibly non-identical ring-like \(r\)-band
source expectations. The second shows each expectation added to the same
bright central survey galaxy. The third shows photon draw 0, where coupled
source-photon fluctuations are visible around the shared foreground scene.

## Conditional recovery

A line rises from near zero recovery at 0.5 arcsec to about 6.2 per cent at
2.5 arcsec, then declines modestly. A shaded simultaneous interval surrounds
the line.

## Conditional PSF–depth–radius response

Nine panels show recovery curves over eleven Einstein radii for the occupied
combinations of five PSF values and two depth values. The PSF 1.05, depth
24.0 combination is absent from the design.

## Bounded brightness response

Two panels compare expectation and photon-realized images over intrinsic
source magnitudes 18.5 to 25.5. Detection fractions reach their maximum at
20.5 and are zero at 24.5 and 25.5. Mean logits decline toward fainter
magnitudes. The photon and expectation curves are close but not identical.

## Non-detections in the selected-system endpoint

Four square Legacy Survey colour cutouts arranged in one row. Each title
identifies a confirmed system and shows its logit below the common threshold.
The images contain bright foreground objects and surrounding structures with
varied colour and configuration; no visual feature is labelled as the cause
of non-detection.
