The observed payload ID, target payload ID, final match row,
theta_E=1.5 parent row, and pair-plan identity agree in all three cases.
Target cutouts are centred on their recorded catalogue coordinates. The
assigned SIE centres include the small registered offsets listed in
`canonical_component_checks.csv`.

## DESI-073.5286-10.2227

- Reference: `0734m102_4599`; matched target: `0736m020_870`.
- The reference cutout and catalogue position agree to
  0.0061 arcsec.
- The matching measurements belong to the central Tractor `DEV`
  component at that recorded coordinate: shape_r=3.789
  arcsec, n=4.000, q=0.269.
- The field has 5 recorded neighbours and
  independent provenance identifies a cluster-lens system. The recorded centre
  therefore need not be the visually brightest concentration in the complete
  cutout.

The measurements describe the recorded central catalogue component, not a
deblended physical-cluster deflector.
